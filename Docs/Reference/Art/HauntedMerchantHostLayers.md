## Authored motion

MerchantHost.asset and MerchantHost.prefab reference one 48 x 84 deformation mesh, MerchantBreathMesh.asset (4,165 vertices), shared by all five layers. Its single `Breath` shape is one smooth displacement field over the native source frame. Because every layer uses the same frame and the same field, overlapping layers move identically at every point: nothing moves as a rigid part and no seam or gap opens. `Tools/Presentation/author-merchant-presentation.py` authors the mesh and timelines; no merchant controller or runtime animation system was added.

| Field region (native source pixels, top-left origin) | Lift at full weight |
| --- | --- |
| Shoulders, chest and upper arms above y760 | 6 pixels (about 1% of the figure), with up to 1.4 pixels of lateral chest spread |
| Head, hat and the hand under the cheek (soft ellipses) | 70% of the shoulder lift, uniform across the face |
| Rows y760..950 | Smooth fade to zero |
| Forearms, elbows and hands on the table (y950 and below) | None |

The 13.8-second `Living still` idle loop holds three breaths of 4.4, 4.9 and 4.5 seconds at slightly different depths: an eased inhale over 40% of the breath, a brief turn, a longer exhale and a short pause at rest. The rest pose is the exhaled source registration. Three blinks fall at 1.55, 5.45 and 11.25 seconds. Arrival, Reaction, Victory, Defeat and Farewell keep their three-second durations and add a whole-body breath over the continuing idle; entry weights blend from an interrupted pose and every cue ends at rest. Reaction is a quick intake that settles slightly below rest and recovers, Victory a relieved breath, Defeat a sigh, and Arrival and Farewell short settles. Method. `Tools/Presentation/cut-merchant-props.py` (numpy and Pillow only) rebuilds every installed file from the plates and the generated donors in `Docs/work/merchant-props`. Each piece's silhouette is declared as vector primitives (ellipses, polygons, stroked lines) and rasterised with 8x supersampling for anti-aliased alpha. For each of three regions (pots, bottle, trunk) built-in imagegen edited a Lanczos 1024 x 1536 upscale of an unlit-plate crop (`targets/`, prompts in `prompts/`): remove the pan, pot and cage (`pots-remove-all-1.png`), remove only the pan and complete what it covers (`pots-remove-pan-1.png`), remove the bottle (`bottle-remove-1.png`) and remove the trunk lid (`trunk-lid-remove-1.png`). Generated images are never installed wholesale. Each donor is registered to the plate with an affine fit on high-passed luminance around the props, props excluded (normalized cross-correlation 0.892, 0.983, 0.994 and 0.984; parameters in `registered/`). Only pixels inside the declared masks are transferred: the donor's fine detail is scaled to the plate's local detail level, its low-frequency colour is matched to the surrounding plate, and the transfer blends linearly from full weight within one pixel of the silhouette to zero at the dilated mask edge. Under the lid the patched plates show the open trunk: the body's front rim along the lid seam and a dark interior, so a lifted lid opens a dark gap.

The lantern-lit patch is derived from the unlit patch, not generated separately: a masked local linear model lit = a * unlit + b per channel, fitted on the plate pixels around each mask (guided-filter style, coarse to fine), predicts the lit plate inside the masks, so both patched plates carry the same reconstruction under the lantern's existing contribution. Cutout pixels come from the respective plate. At partial-coverage edges colours are un-premultiplied against the layers below, so compositing the pieces back over the patched plates reproduces the installed plates. The pot's upper-left and ring, hidden behind the pan, are completed from the remove-pan donor (353 pixels, `masks/HangingPot-completed.png`; its lit colour uses the same model fitted on the pot's visible pixels). No other region is completed.

Verification (script output, also in props.json). The installed plates keep their SHA-256 hashes. ## Relighting sources

The textures under `Dice-Unity/Assets/Dice/Art/HauntedMerchant/Relight` let the merchant, the canopy cloth and the hanging cup be relit by a lantern that can switch off and a candle that moves. The painted merchant art carries warm lantern light from the upper left and warm candle light from the front and below; these textures separate it into a cool night-ambient base, a lantern-lit reference and data maps for computed candle light. `Tools/Presentation/author-merchant-relight.py` (Python 3.10+, numpy, Pillow, scipy) reproduces every installed texture from the preserved donors; `build --replace --save-registered` rebuilds byte-identical files and keeps existing .meta GUIDs. Measurements are in `Docs/work/merchant-relight/relight-build.json`; prompts, edit targets, every candidate donor and the registered donors are in `Docs/work/merchant-relight`.

### Shader conventions

- Lighting pairs follow SceneLighting.shader in linear light: lantern 1 = unlit + max(lanternlit - unlit, 0) * tint * response scale; lantern 0 = unlit. For every merchant layer, for the canopy and for the cup, lanternlit >= unlit holds in every channel of every pixel (0 violations measured), so lantern 1 at full intensity reproduces the lantern-lit texture exactly and, for the canopy, the painted cloth.
- Depth: R = 0 farthest from the viewer (torso back plane), R = 1 nearest (front-most hands and forearms on the table). It is authored procedurally from the existing masks and normalized so the merchant alpha spans exactly 0..255: a torso plane with a chest bulge; the head (face and hat bulges) forward of the chest, blended into the torso over a 10 px Gaussian so the collar and brim seams are smooth; the image-left sleeve rising from the shoulder to the elbow on the table; the resting arm, continuous with the torso at the shoulder, rising to its elbow and to the hand on the table (nearest); and the bare supporting forearm from its elbow on the table (near) to the hand under the cheek (in front of the face). A slight recession over 28 px inside the silhouette rounds the outer edges. The only steep steps (at most 42 levels between neighbouring pixels) lie on the bare forearm outlines where a forearm sits in front of the torso or face. Outside the merchant alpha the field is extended from the nearest silhouette pixel and blurred, so bilinear sampling at the edge is stable.
- Lantern response: MerchantLanternResponse.png R is an elliptical linear distance clamped at 1, R = min(1, hypot((x - 90) / 650, (y - 335) / 950)) in continuous background pixels (pixel centers at +0.5); a fit over every unclamped pixel reproduces the installed map within 0.5/255. G is the lantern source glow. Through the merchant mapping x = 0.9136 * spriteX + 21, y = 0.94 * spriteY + 28.8, the lantern sits at (75.525, 325.745) in the merchant frame with radii (711.471, 1010.638) merchant pixels; MerchantLanternResponseHost.png evaluates that ellipse in R with G = B = 0. Inside the merchant alpha it differs from the background map sampled through the mapping by at most 0.99/255 (mean 0.11/255). Like the background map, it is a 2D image-plane distance and does not use depth.
- Canopy response: MerchantLanternResponse.png shares the canopy frame and covers the cloth correctly as is: R on the cloth spans 44..213 and matches the analytic ellipse within 0.5/255, and G (lantern glow) is 0 on every cloth pixel. No separate canopy response map exists.

### Method

All transfers follow the masked-transfer method above: generated images are registered to the native frame and only pixels inside a declared mask change. Each donor was generated with built-in imagegen from an edit target that composites the art to be relit over the matching lighting plate at its registered placement (merchant-frame crop (0, 240)-(760, 1100), canopy background-frame crop (0, 0)-(720, 520), both upscaled 2x). Registration fits an affine map by normalised cross-correlation of locally contrast-normalised, high-passed luminance inside the eroded region, then adds a smooth residual displacement from 40 px tile correlation peaks; it follows painted structure, not illumination.

The illumination change is a linear-light ratio map, ratio = G(w * donor) / G(w * source) per channel, with a 3 px Gaussian G over the region interior w (alpha > 250 eroded 3 px), extended past the silhouette from the nearest interior value. Relit color is source * ratio, so the painted detail, folds and identity are the source's own; only illumination differs. Two limits handle painted lantern glints and rim strokes thinner than the ratio scale: each channel is capped at 1.15 times the donor's local maximum (9 px window), and saturation (max - min) / max is capped at 1.1 times the donor's local maximum saturation (floor 0.08) by blending toward the pixel's own luminance. Lantern-lit pixels take the per-channel maximum of the lantern and unlit results.

Per-layer textures use the MerchantHost-frame maps, so wherever a layer's source pixel equals MerchantHost.png outside its repair mask, its output equals the host output (219,954 head and 176,964 torso pixels checked, 0 differ). Reconstructed concealed regions (MerchantHeadRepairMask.png, 2,647 px; MerchantTorsoAnimationRepairMask.png, 45,637 px) have no visible counterpart in MerchantHost, whose pixels there show the hand or forearm. Inside those masks the ratio and cap maps are a harmonic (Laplace) fill in log space from the surrounding visible pixels: a smooth membrane with no seam at the repair boundary.

The canopy cloth is MerchantOriginal.png sampled through CanopyOriginalOverhangMask.png (the `_LayerMask` of CanopySource.mat and CanopyRoofSource.mat). CanopyMotionMask.png belongs to the retired generated Canopy.png split, has a 941 x 1671 frame and is not used. No candle light reaches the cloth: the candle sits on the table near background y 880..1090, far below the cloth (y 0..406), and the painted cloth shows only lantern warmth (the glowing torn edges) over moonlit color, so CanopyLanternLit.png is the painted cloth unchanged. The unlit donor kept roughly the painted luminance while cooling the hue. Because the lantern only adds light, each relit cloth pixel keeps the donor's hue and is scaled uniformly (scale smoothed over 2 px) to fit under the painted value, then limited per channel. The unlit cloth averages 40% of the painted luminance, close to the merchant's unlit-to-painted ratio (44%).

The hanging cup is relit in its own 1024 x 1536 frame by `author-merchant-relight.py cup-targets` and `cup`, which write only the two cup textures. Its edit targets show the cup over the scene it hangs in, resampled at its rest rect: the props-removed plate, the hanging pot and pan cutouts and the canopy cloth (unlit plate, cutouts and CanopyUnlit.png for the unlit target; lantern plate, cutouts and CanopyLanternLit.png for the lantern target). The unlit donor relights the painted cup; the lantern donor adds the lantern to the unlit result over the lantern scene, and HangingCupLanternLit.png takes the per-channel maximum of the lantern and unlit results, as for the merchant. Registration and the ratio, highlight and saturation caps are the merchant's, with the ratio Gaussian at 6 px because the cup is drawn at about 1/8 of its native size. Painted lantern warmth on HangingCup.png (amber on the left of the body, rim and mouth) is removed; the unlit cup averages 53% of the painted luminance. The lantern sits far to the upper left (MerchantLanternResponse.png R is 192..244 over the cup), so its contribution is modest: it warms the left of the body, rim and rope and leaves the right side and handle in the night ambient. Lantern-lit over unlit averages 1.52, 1.09 and 1.01 in linear R, G and B, between the hanging pot (1.47, 1.19, 1.07) and the hanging pan (2.16, 1.34, 0.97) beside it.
