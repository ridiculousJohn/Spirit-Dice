## Authored motion

MerchantHost.asset and MerchantHost.prefab reference one 48 x 84 deformation mesh, MerchantBreathMesh.asset (4,165 vertices), shared by all five layers. Its single `Breath` shape is one smooth displacement field over the native source frame. Because every layer uses the same frame and the same field, overlapping layers move identically at every point: nothing moves as a rigid part and no seam or gap opens. `Tools/Presentation/author-merchant-presentation.py` authors the mesh and timelines; no merchant controller or runtime animation system was added.

| Field region (native source pixels, top-left origin) | Lift at full weight |
| --- | --- |
| Shoulders, chest and upper arms above y760 | 6 pixels (about 1% of the figure), with up to 1.4 pixels of lateral chest spread |
| Head, hat and the hand under the cheek (soft ellipses) | 70% of the shoulder lift, uniform across the face |
| Rows y760..950 | Smooth fade to zero |
| Forearms, elbows and hands on the table (y950 and below) | None |

The 13.8-second `Living still` idle loop holds three breaths of 4.4, 4.9 and 4.5 seconds at slightly different depths: an eased inhale over 40% of the breath, a brief turn, a longer exhale and a short pause at rest. The rest pose is the exhaled source registration. Three blinks fall at 1.55, 5.45 and 11.25 seconds. Arrival, Reaction, Victory, Defeat and Farewell keep their three-second durations and add a whole-body breath over the continuing idle; entry weights blend from an interrupted pose and every cue ends at rest. Reaction is a quick intake that settles slightly below rest and recovers, Victory a relieved breath, Defeat a sigh, and Arrival and Farewell short settles. The earlier MerchantHeadMesh, MerchantRestingArmMesh and MerchantSupportingArmMesh assets (nod, tilt, lift and release shapes) are no longer referenced.
