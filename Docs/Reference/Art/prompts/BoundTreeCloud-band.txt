Use case: illustration-story (transparent element for compositing)
Asset type: transparent cloud layer for a 2D game scene (painted digital illustration, moonlit night sky over a swamp); it is drawn in the dark blue night sky behind a huge black tree and its branches, scrolls slowly sideways across the full moon and repeats
Primary request: Paint a single wide horizontal band of soft, dim night clouds on a genuinely transparent background (real alpha channel, not a painted checkerboard and not a flat colour), matching the clouds around the moon in the reference exactly in colour, softness and value.
Input images: Image 1: reference only (style, colour and lighting of the sky the clouds will drift through; do not reproduce its content)
Subject: dim, low-contrast night cloud banks like the ones behind the branches in Image 1: one long, broad, flattened bank of soft layered cloud and one smaller, thinner, stretched wisp, both drifting horizontally. Smooth, softly blended forms with gently rounded upper edges catching faint moonlight and darker, diffuse undersides that dissolve gradually into transparency; the cloud value is only a little lighter than a dark blue night sky. Between and around the two masses there is a wide stretch of completely clear, fully transparent sky, at least a third of the width, so that when the band drifts past the moon the moon is fully clear part of the time. Composition/framing: landscape 3:2; the band runs horizontally through roughly the middle half of the height, fully transparent at the top and bottom of the image; the left and right edges of the image are both fully transparent (the clear gap of the band continues across the image edge), so that copies placed side by side join without a seam; no single striking feature that would be noticed when it repeats.
Lighting/mood: cool dim moonlight as in Image 1: muted slate blue-grey clouds, the moonlit upper rims a soft pale grey-blue, never white and never bright, no glow source and no moon in the image.
Style/medium: identical painterly digital illustration style, the same smooth airbrushed softness as the clouds around the moon in Image 1
Avoid: puffy bright cumulus; curly tendrils; grainy, noisy or fluffy cotton texture; hard or sharp edges; bright white; moon, trees, branches, hanging moss, ground, horizon, stars, sky background colour or any scenery; a checkerboard pattern; text or watermark
